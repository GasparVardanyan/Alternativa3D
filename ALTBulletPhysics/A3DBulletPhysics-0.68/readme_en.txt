A3DBulletPhysics © redefy 23-11-2011
This is a prot of physics engine (BulletPhysics (c++) > AwayPhysics (by muzer with Alchemy, as3) > A3DBulletPhysics (by redefy, as3)).

Features: 
 Collision Shapes:
  BoxShape
  , BvhTriangleMeshShape
  , CapsuleShape
  , CompoundShape
  , ConeShape
  , ConvexHullShape
  , CylinderShape
  , HeightfieldTerrainShape
  , SphereShape
  and StaticPlaneShape.
 3D Objects: Box, GeoSphere, Plane from Alternativa3D 8 and Additional Objects:
  Capsule
  , Cone
  , Cylinder
  , RegularPolygon
  and Terrain.
 Algorithms: GJK (Gilbert Johnson Keerthi) and EPA (Expanded Polytope Algorithm).
 Axis-Aligned Bounding Boxes Swept Collision Test.
 Discrete and Continuous Collision Detection.
 Soft Body support: cloth, rope and deformable objects.
 Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics.
 Constraints:
  ConeTwistConstraint
  , Generic6DofConstraint
  , HingeConstraint
  and Point2PointConstraint.
 Multithreading.
 Cross-Platform.
